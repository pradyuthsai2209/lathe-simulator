⚙ MODEL CONFIGURATION & ENGINEERING SPECIFICATIONS

Specifications verified against published HMT Machine Tools Limited heavy-duty lathe documentation. Estimated or standard industrial values are clearly distinguished and can be edited below.
Cutting Speed (Vc)
Vc = (π · D · N_spindle) / 1000 [m/min]
Surface linear velocity at tool tip. D in mm, N in RPM.
Kienzle Cutting Force (Fc)
Fc = kc1.1 · ap · f^(1 - mc) · (1 - γ/100) · K_wear · K_cool [N]
Tangential cutting force with tool rake angle γ, flank wear, and coolant lubrication factors.
Spindle Cutting Torque (Mc)
Mc = Fc · (D / 2000) [N·m]
Rotational moment exerted on workpiece and spindle bearings.
Spindle Cutting Power (Pc)
Pc = (Fc · Vc) / 60000 [kW]
Net mechanical cutting power absorbed at the shear plane.
Motor Electrical Power (Pe)
Pe = ((Pc / η_mech) + P_tare) / η_elec [kW]
Total 3-phase grid power accounting for headstock gear train friction and motor efficiency.
3-Phase Line Current (I)
I = (Pe · 1000) / (√3 · VL · cos φ) [A]
Line current at nominal 415V supply with dynamic power factor cos φ.
Material Removal Rate (MRR)
MRR = Vc · ap · f [cm³/min]
Volumetric stock reduction rate per minute.
Loewen & Shaw Flash Temperature
ΔT = (0.38 · kc / ρCp) · (Vc · f / α)^0.33 [°C]
Cutting zone interface temperature rise based on workpiece thermal diffusivity α.

  • Regenerative Chatter & Vibration: ISO 10816 vibration severity evaluated from baseline unbalance plus dynamic cutting force amplification when tool overhang or depth of cut exceeds stability boundaries.
